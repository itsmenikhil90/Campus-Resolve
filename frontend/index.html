From complaint to resolution

  1. 1

    You describe the problem

    Plain language is fine. Add a photo or document if it helps make the case.

  2. 2

    The system reads and routes it

    Keywords in your description are matched against departments, so it reaches the right desk without you having to pick one.

  3. 3

    An administrator takes it up

    Every complaint gets a ticket number. Sensitive matters are flagged for confidential handling.

  4. 4

    You watch it move, and close it

    Track the status any time with your ticket number. You're asked to confirm once it's resolved.
